Strategy

  1. Understand the business, the market and your capacity before recommending a single channel.
  2. Set up the site, tracking and CRM the plan needs, so every lead is captured and attributed.
  3. Create demand in the channels your buyers actually use, online and offline, under one plan.
  4. Follow-up that turns attention into booked jobs, so paid demand doesn't leak between the call and the estimate.
  5. Track the journey from first touch to booked job to revenue, in one report.
  6. Improve what the data shows: fix the step where jobs are lost and move budget to what books work.
  7. Scale what works: reviews, referrals, retention and new markets on the same system.
