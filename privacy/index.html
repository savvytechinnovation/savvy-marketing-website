02 What we collect

In short

Only what you type into our forms, plus the basic technical data any website receives.

We collect personal information when you choose to send it to us through one of these forms:

  • Contact form: name, email, company, website, the services you are interested in, budget range and your message.
  • Fit check / assessment: your answers about your business (industry, size, current marketing, goals) and the contact details you provide so we can follow up.
  • Partnership enquiry: business details, location, service area and contact details.
  • Job applications: name, email, phone, links (e.g. LinkedIn or portfolio), your answers and the CV / résumé file you upload.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type and the pages requested, recorded in our hosting provider’s server logs.

03 How we use it

In short

To answer you, prepare for a conversation, consider a job application or send the newsletter you asked for.

We use form submissions only for the purpose of the form: replying to an enquiry, preparing for a consultation, assessing a partnership fit, reviewing an application for a role, or sending the newsletter. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Your request, our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries, and your consent for the newsletter.
