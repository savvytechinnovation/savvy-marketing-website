How we measure

How results are labelled

No vanity metrics. Every number says where it came from, for how long, and what it was before.

  1. ●Measured in CRM

    Jobs and revenue from the client’s own CRM. Can be used as a headline result.

  2. ●Platform-reported

    From Google Ads, Local Services Ads or call tracking. Can be a headline, and always says which platform.

  3. ●Estimated

    Modelled or partial data. Only inside a case, with a one-line explanation. Never a headline.

  4. ●Always shown

    The period (e.g. Jan–Sep 2026) and the “before” value. No percentages without the base numbers.
